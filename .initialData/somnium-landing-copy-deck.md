# Somnium: Landing Page Copy Deck

Built from a teardown of Eight Sleep, Beam, Rise Science, and the Romanian pharmacy channel.
Copy is Romanian-first. English glosses in brackets where useful for review.

---

## Part 1: What the competitors do, and what transfers

### Eight Sleep (eightsleep.com)

| Device | What it is | Transfers? |
|---|---|---|
| Identity positioning | "Sleep is your edge." Sells performance, not rest | Partially. Too athletic for the RO wellness buyer |
| Clinical percentage block | Up to 44% less time to fall asleep, 34% more deep sleep, 45% less snoring, 23% fewer wake-ups | **No.** EU claim law blocks this for supplements |
| Sleeper-type grid | Six blocks: couples, menopausal women, wellness seekers, hot sleepers, athletes, snorers. Each names a specific nightly experience | **Yes. Strongest single device on the page** |
| Advisory board | Huberman, Matthew Walker | Yes, but with Romanian names. Huberman means nothing to a 45-year-old in Cluj |
| Risk reversal above the fold | 30 nights risk-free, free returns, 0% financing | Yes, adapted |
| Testimonials as short quoted arcs | "Within 3 weeks I started to have big improvements in REM and deep sleep" | Yes |

### Beam (shopbeam.com)

| Device | What it is | Transfers? |
|---|---|---|
| Ritual over pill | "The ritual that changed bedtime." Warm cocoa, frother, 30 to 45 minutes before bed | Yes, and it's underused in RO |
| Ingredient-to-stage mapping | Each ingredient tied to a phase: apigenin and magnesium for drifting off, L-theanine for the transition into deep sleep, reishi for physical repair, GABA for REM | **Yes. This is education that sells** |
| Melatonin-free variant | Sold as a headline option, not a footnote | Yes. Directly answers the dependency objection |
| Subscription framing | Monthly delivery framed as consistency, not lock-in | Yes |

### Rise Science (risescience.com)

| Device | What it is | Transfers? |
|---|---|---|
| Own one concept | Sleep debt. Everything on the page routes through it | **Yes, but pick your own concept** |
| Attack the incumbent frame | Explicitly tells you to stop chasing the eight hour rule and to ignore deep and REM scores from wearables | Yes. Very effective, very cheap |
| Day-one payoff | Tells you your number on day one, before you change anything | **Yes. This is the quiz** |

### Romanian channel (Catena, Farmacia Tei, DrMax, Dona, Help Net, eMAG)

Same molecules, same copy, no brand. Melatonin plus valerian plus passiflora plus magnesium plus B6, described in flat pharmaceutical register. eMAG filters show roughly 346 melatonin listings, 106 valerian, 94 magnesium. Zenyth already runs a stres-somn category.

Nobody in the channel asks why you can't sleep. They all sell the molecule first. That gap is the entire opportunity.

## Part 2: The positioning decision

**Don't compete on formula. Compete on diagnosis.**

Rise owns "sleep debt." Beam owns "the ritual." Eight Sleep owns "sleep as performance edge." Somnium should own **cauza** (the cause).

Core promise, in one line:

> Nu toate insomniile sunt la fel. A ta are o cauză. Găsește-o în 3 minute.
> *[Not all insomnia is the same. Yours has a cause. Find it in 3 minutes.]*

This does three useful things at once. It sidesteps EU health claim restrictions, because you're promising identification rather than an outcome. It makes the quiz the product rather than a lead magnet. And it reframes every previous failed attempt the visitor has had as a matching problem, not a personal failure, which is the single most common reason people give up on sleep supplements.

**Claims constraint.** Under EU Reg. 1924/2006 you are limited to authorised claims. Melatonin has an authorised claim around reducing time taken to fall asleep at roughly 1mg, and a separate jet-lag claim at 0.5mg. Magnesium and B6 have claims around normal nervous system function and reduction of tiredness. Botanicals such as valerian and passiflora sit in the on-hold category with uneven national enforcement. **Verify all exact permitted wording against the EU Register of nutrition and health claims before publishing.** I have not confirmed the current wording and it has been revised over time.

## Part 3: Page architecture

Twelve blocks, in order. Each has a job. If a block doesn't have a job, cut it.

---

### 1. Hero

**Job:** name the visitor's exact nightly experience, then offer identification instead of a product.

```
[eyebrow]
SOMNIUM

[H1]
Nu dormi prost.
Dormi prost dintr-un motiv anume.

[sub]
Insomnia de stres, cea hormonală și cea de ritm circadian
au aceleași simptome la 3 dimineața și tratamente complet diferite.
Testul nostru de 3 minute îți spune care e a ta.

[CTA primar]
Află-ți tipul de somn

[CTA secundar]
Vezi cum funcționează

[trust strip, sub CTA]
Fără abonament forțat · Livrare în 48h · Formulat de specialiști în medicină funcțională
```

**Note on the H1.** The line works because it refuses the visitor's self-blame. Most people arriving on a sleep page have already decided the problem is them.

Alternative H1 if you want the higher-arousal version:

```
Te-ai trezit iar la 3:14.
Nu e întâmplare. E un tipar.
```

### 2. The cost block

**Job:** this is the "convince them sleep matters" section you asked for. It has to hurt without being a lecture. Concrete, everyday, and specific to the last 24 hours rather than to a 30-year mortality curve. Nobody buys on mortality curves.

```
[H2]
Ce ți-a luat noaptea trecută

[four cards, each: icon, stat-free headline, one line]

Deciziile de dimineață
După o noapte scurtă, creierul trece pe pilot automat.
Alegi mai repede și mai prost, și nu simți diferența.

Răbdarea
Amigdala reacționează disproporționat când ai dormit puțin.
Nu ești irascibil. Ești nedormit.

Pofta de zahăr la ora 16
Grelina crește, leptina scade. Corpul cere energie rapidă
pentru că nu a primit-o din odihnă.

Ce ai învățat ieri
Consolidarea memoriei se face în somnul profund.
O noapte pierdută înseamnă o zi pe care nu o păstrezi.

[closing line, full width, smaller]
Nimic din toate astea nu se vede pe termen scurt.
Se acumulează. Și apoi devine felul în care trăiești.
```

**Why no statistics here.** Every Romanian pharmacy site already lists "afectează concentrarea, imunitatea, metabolismul." It's wallpaper. Specific lived moments beat percentages, and they carry zero regulatory risk.

If you do want one number for credibility, pick a single well-documented one, attribute it precisely, and use it once. Don't stack four.

[H3 sub]
Patru tipare acoperă majoritatea insomniilor.
Fiecare cere altceva.

[cards, image + title + symptom + mechanism hint]

**Implementation note.** Each card links to the relevant archetype landing page. This is your organic search surface and it maps to the article topic JSON you already have.

### 4. The reframe block

**Job:** Rise's move. Attack the incumbent frame, cheaply and directly.

```
[H2]
De ce nu au funcționat până acum

[three short paragraphs, no bullets]

Ai luat melatonină și nu a mers.
Melatonina mută ceasul biologic. Dacă problema ta e cortizolul crescut seara,
melatonina nu are ce să rezolve. Nu era produsul greșit. Era diagnosticul greșit.

Ai încercat magneziu și tot te trezeai la 3.
Magneziul susține sistemul nervos, dar nu stabilizează glicemia nocturnă.
Dacă te trezești mereu la aceeași oră, cauza e metabolică, nu musculară.

Ai citit că îți trebuie 8 ore.
Necesarul individual variază. Mai important decât numărul de ore
este consistența orei de culcare și adâncimea somnului.
```

**Why this works.** It's the most trust-generating block on the page because it explains their failure without selling. It also pre-frames the quiz as necessary rather than a marketing device.

### 5. How it works

**Job:** collapse the perceived effort. Three steps, no more.

```
[H2]
Trei pași

01 · Testul
3 minute. 12 întrebări despre cum arată de fapt noaptea ta.
Fără cont, fără email obligatoriu ca să vezi rezultatul.

02 · Protocolul
Primești tipul tău, cauza probabilă, și protocolul potrivit:
produs, doză, oră de administrare, și ce schimbi în rutină.

03 · Ajustarea
După 3 săptămâni reevaluăm. Dacă tiparul s-a schimbat, se schimbă și protocolul.
```

**Critical decision: don't gate the quiz result behind email.** Gating kills the trust the whole page was built to establish, and the RO market is unusually allergic to it. Capture email on the follow-up ("îți trimitem protocolul complet") instead. This also keeps your GDPR consent story clean given the wellness-data sensitivity you already flagged in the architecture.

### 6. The night map

**Job:** Beam's ingredient-to-stage mapping. This is education that sells, because it makes the visitor feel they now understand their own night.

```
[H2]
Ce se întâmplă între 23:00 și 07:00

[horizontal timeline, four segments]

23:00 → 23:30 · ADORMIREA
Temperatura corpului scade, melatonina urcă.
Dacă acest pas e blocat, nimic din ce urmează nu se întâmplă la timp.

23:30 → 02:00 · SOMNUL PROFUND
Reparație fizică, hormon de creștere, consolidarea memoriei.
Cea mai mare parte se întâmplă în prima jumătate a nopții.

02:00 → 05:00 · FEREASTRA FRAGILĂ
Cortizolul începe să urce. Glicemia scade.
Aici se rupe somnul la majoritatea oamenilor.

05:00 → 07:00 · REM
Procesare emoțională, învățare, visele lungi.
Se pierde primul când te culci târziu sau bei alcool seara.
```

Under each segment, name which of your SKUs supports that phase. That converts a product grid into a protocol.

### 7. Proof without outcome claims

**Job:** replace Eight Sleep's percentage block with something legally clean and arguably more persuasive in a low-trust supplement market.

```
[H2]
Ce poți verifica

Doze afișate integral
Nicio formulă proprietară. Vezi exact câte miligrame din fiecare ingredient,
per porție, pe ambalaj și pe site.

Forme biodisponibile
Bisglicinat, nu oxid. Costă mai mult și se absoarbe mai bine.
Diferența contează mai mult decât cantitatea de pe etichetă.

Fără doze decorative
Dacă un ingredient e sub pragul studiat, nu îl punem ca să arate bine pe listă.

Analize per lot
Buletinul de analiză pentru lotul tău e accesibil după codul de pe cutie.
```

**This is your real differentiator against the pharmacy shelf**, where proprietary blends and magnesium oxide are the norm. It's checkable, which means it's credible, which is exactly what the Romanian supplement buyer is short of.

### 8. Authority

**Job:** Eight Sleep's advisory board, localised.

Huberman and Walker carry no weight with the Romanian mainstream buyer. Use a Romanian somnologist or a physician with functional medicine credentials, with a real photo, real credentials, and one paragraph in their own voice about why they were willing to put their name on it. One credible local name beats three imported ones.

If you don't have that person yet, this block waits. An empty authority block is worse than no authority block.

---

### 9. Objection handling

**Job:** kill the four purchase blockers explicitly. Accordion format.

```
"Nu vreau să devin dependent de melatonină."
Melatonina nu creează dependență fiziologică. Creează însă obișnuință
psihologică dacă e folosită ca soluție permanentă. De aceea protocoalele
noastre sunt pe termen limitat, cu reevaluare, și de aceea avem
formule fără melatonină pentru cauzele care nu țin de ritm.

"Am mai încercat suplimente și nu a mers."
Probabil ai încercat produsul potrivit pentru altă cauză. Din cele patru
tipare de mai sus, un singur tip de formulă funcționează pentru fiecare.
Testul există exact ca să nu repeți experimentul.

"E mai scump decât ce găsesc la farmacie."
Da. Compară doza și forma chimică, nu prețul cutiei.
Calculatorul de mai jos îți arată costul pe porție și pe miligram activ.

"Nu ar trebui să discut cu medicul meu?"
Ba da, și îți dăm ce ai nevoie pentru asta: protocolul complet, dozele,
și interacțiunile cunoscute, într-un PDF pe care îl poți duce la consultație.
```

That last one is counterintuitive and worth keeping. Inviting medical scrutiny is the strongest trust signal available to a supplement brand, and almost nobody does it.

### 10. Testimonials

**Job:** arcs, not adjectives.

Selection rule: every testimonial must contain a specific before-state, a specific timeframe, and a specific after-state. "Produs excelent, recomand" is worse than nothing because it reads as purchased.

Target shape:

```
"Mă trezeam la 3 și jumătate în fiecare noapte, de vreo doi ani.
După test mi-a ieșit tiparul metabolic, ceva ce nu îmi trecuse prin cap.
A treia săptămână a fost prima în care am dormit până la 6."
— Prenume, 41 ani, București
```

Segment them by archetype and let the visitor filter, mirroring Eight Sleep's tabbed review wall (hot sleepers / couples / troubled sleepers). Seeing three people with *your* pattern converts better than thirty generic five-star lines.

---

### 11. Risk reversal

**Job:** remove the last friction. Put it immediately above the final CTA and repeat one element in the hero trust strip.

```
30 de nopți. Dacă nu simți diferența, îți returnăm banii.
Fără formular, fără să trimiți produsul înapoi.
```

Also state plainly: no forced subscription, cancel in one click. The RO market has been burned repeatedly by dark-pattern subscription flows and saying this outright is worth real conversion.

[CTA]
Fă testul · 3 minute

[under]

## Part 4: Things not to copy

**Outcome percentages.** Eight Sleep's 44% and 34% are device claims under a different regulatory regime. Replicating that pattern for a food supplement in the EU is a compliance problem and, separately, a credibility problem the moment a customer checks.

**Imported authority figures.** Huberman and Walker are inside-baseball for the Romanian mainstream. They signal "translated American marketing," which cuts against you.

**Ritual-first positioning.** Beam's cocoa ritual is charming and it's their entire brand. It's also weaker in a market where the buyer wants a fix, not a lifestyle, and where hot chocolate before bed reads as indulgence rather than health. Keep ritual as a secondary layer, not the hero.

**An eight-archetype grid on the homepage.** You have eight defined. Show four. The other four live on the quiz result pages, where the visitor has already self-selected and choice paralysis no longer applies.

---

## Part 5: Build order

If you're sequencing implementation:

1. Hero, archetype grid, quiz entry point. This is the minimum viable page and it tests the core positioning bet on its own.
2. Cost block and reframe block. These carry the persuasion load.
3. Night map and proof block. These carry the differentiation load.
4. Authority block. Blocked on finding the right person. Don't ship a placeholder.
5. Testimonials. Blocked on having real ones with the right shape. Don't fabricate, and don't ship generic ones as filler.

The page works without 4 and 5. It does not work without 1.
